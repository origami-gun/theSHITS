Google Drive sync

Expiration notifications

Email reminders A weekly list every Sunday at 7 AM of anything expiring in the next 7 days, plus a final reminder the morning before each item expires. They're sent by a small script in your own Google account that reads your synced Drive data, so they arrive even when this app is closed. Items you mark as used are skipped. The script also emails when a subscribed item runs low (with add-to-cart links; see Shopping), and files every reminder under a "The S.H.I.T.S." label in Gmail.

Warning windows

Set how many days before an item's expiration date it should start counting as "expiring soon." There's one default for everything, plus optional overrides per category.

Shopping list

Only fills your cart. It can never check out.

The app doesn't sign in to Amazon or Walmart and has no payment details. Walmart's button puts the checked items in your Walmart cart; Amazon's opens each product page so you tap “Add to Cart” there (Amazon no longer lets outside apps fill a cart). Nothing is bought until one of you reviews the cart and places the order. Items you send to a cart are marked “In cart” for 2 weeks (or until restocked) so they aren't added twice.

Categories

Each category's color is used to mark its items in the inventory. Renaming a category updates every item in it; removing one moves its items to “Other”.
